Bundled resources STV/Resources/hgv5sign.js (signature-only) and
STV/Resources/hongguo_app.js (full in-app playback engine) are taken
from the THLRedGuo "official-source" hongguo.js CatPawOpen spider
(version 1.1.0-dev.23):

  App signing and Spade: jackotom/hongguo-drama 84b6e88,
  via N3urda/hongguoTV eea73e6 and woshishiq1/drpys 22261ad.
  THL adaptation: device fetch transport, browser crypto/Buffer,
  bounded episode recovery.
  Bundled Buffer, CryptoJS and URL-Parse retain their MIT notices.

It is loaded into the app's QuickJS sandbox at runtime and only used to
produce request signatures for the Hongguo (fqnovel) playback endpoints.
